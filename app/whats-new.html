What’s new

The changes in each version of INR Notebook, newest first.

Version 1.5

6 November 2026

  • A fresh look: a new typeface, softer colours and a floating tab bar.
  • Home shows your day as a timeline: today’s warfarin, your meals and anything due today.
  • The next INR test and clinic visit show on Home in the week before them.
  • Warfarin: the week as a strip of days, and a month calendar in your dose history.
  • The INR chart’s period sits above it, and a bar shows how many results were below, in and above your target.
  • Health diary: a Vitals tab for blood pressure, pulse, SpO2, temperature and weight. Swipe between the diary’s tabs, and add from one button.
  • Updated terms of use, to agree to again in the app.

Version 1.4

5 November 2026

  • INR Notebook is now in Kiswahili, Français, Español and Português, as well as English.
  • It follows your phone’s language, or choose another from the menu, in Settings or on the first screen.
  • The terms of use in each of these languages.
  • The danger signs from your warfarin card and the clinic’s food list stay exactly as written.
  • Dates are written the way your language writes them.

Version 1.3

27 October 2026

  • The terms of use, to read and agree to in the app.
  • A short tour of the app.
  • Share INR Notebook, and rate it on Google Play, from the menu.
