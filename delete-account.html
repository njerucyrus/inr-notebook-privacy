Delete your Smart Scan account — INR Notebook

Last updated: 15 November 2026

INR Notebook needs no account, except for Smart Scan, which reads meal photos and medicine packets. Signing in to Smart Scan with Google creates a Smart Scan account. Deleting it removes:

  • your sign-in (your Google account's name, email address and id, as Firebase Authentication keeps them for INR Notebook)
  • your scan counts: this month's, the scans given back, and any extra scans
  • the record of each scan (which feature, which service answered, and when)

Your health records are not on our server, so there is nothing else to delete there. They stay on your phone, and deleting the Smart Scan account does not touch them.

In the app

  • First, open INR Notebook.
  • Then go to More, then Settings, then Smart Scan.
  • Last, tap Delete this account, and confirm.

Everything listed above is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Write to coolnjeru@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, asking for your Smart Scan account to be deleted. We delete everything listed above within 30 days and reply to say it is done.

Nothing is kept afterwards, except what the law requires us to keep.
